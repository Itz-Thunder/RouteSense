# RouteSense

RouteSense is a hackathon project for safer and more reliable transportation and logistics across Northeast India.

## Purpose

RouteSense helps users plan journeys and deliveries by considering:

- Weather conditions and possible disruptions
- Safer route recommendations
- Road accessibility and travel conditions
- Transportation and logistics needs

The goal is to make navigation across the diverse terrain of Northeast India more informed, dependable, and safe.

## Vision

To simplify route planning with practical recommendations that account for real-world conditions and prioritize safety.
